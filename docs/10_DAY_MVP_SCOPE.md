# 10-DAY MVP SCOPE

P0:
Language selection, simple artisan onboarding, artisan dashboard, photo upload,
voice input, STT, image/product understanding, catalogue generation, AI questions,
price recommendation, optional location, review/edit, artisan approval, multilingual
listing, customer marketplace, natural-language AI search, product details, Meet the
Artisan, demo order, basic admin/impact dashboard.

P1/P2 if time permits:
Voice editing, recommendations, image enhancement, artisan story audio, more languages.

Do not spend prototype time on custom LLM training, blockchain, complex microservices,
full logistics, or production marketplace integrations.
